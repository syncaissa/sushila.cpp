What are the main differences between TCP and UDP? Give examples of when to use each.assistant

**TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) vs UDP (User Datagram Protocol)**

TCP and UDP are two fundamental transport-layer protocols used in the Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP). While both protocols are used for data transfer, they have distinct differences in their design, functionality, and use cases.

**TCP (Transmission Control Protocol)**

* **Connection-oriented**: Establishes a connection between the sender and receiver before data transfer begins.
* **Reliable**: Ensures data is delivered in the correct order, with error-checking and correction mechanisms.
* **Ordered**: Guarantees that data is delivered in the same order it was sent.
